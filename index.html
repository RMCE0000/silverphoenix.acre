About Silver Phoenix ACRE

Silver Phoenix ACRE — A Company Recycling Everything — specializes in the secure destruction of confidential documents through high-temperature incineration. We maintain an unbroken chain of custody from the moment your materials leave your facility to the moment they are reduced to ash. Every step is logged, witnessed, and certified so you can prove compliance with confidence.

Our name reflects our mission: like a phoenix, we transform what was once sensitive information into something safe, letting organizations rise free from liability and risk.

How It Works

  1. 1. Schedule

    Contact us with your volume and security requirements. We design a destruction plan tailored to your needs and compliance obligations.

  2. 2. Secure Collection

    Documents are collected in tamper-evident containers. Every item is logged and tracked under strict chain of custody protocols.

  3. 3. Incinerate

    Materials are destroyed through high-temperature incineration. Nothing is recoverable. You receive a certificate of destruction.

  4. 4. Certify

    A detailed certificate of destruction is issued, documenting every step from collection to ash for your compliance records.
