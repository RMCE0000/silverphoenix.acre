About Silver Phoenix Acre

We are a full-spectrum recycling company dedicated to recovering value from everyday and complex waste streams. From paper and plastics to electronics and mixed materials, our team manages the full journey with transparency, safety, and environmental care.

What We Recycle

Residential Recycling

Curbside support, bulk pickups, and neighborhood collection plans for homes and apartment communities.

Commercial & Office Waste

End-to-end workplace recycling for paper, plastic, packaging, glass, and food-service materials.

E-Waste Recovery

Secure handling of devices, batteries, cables, and components with compliant downstream processing.

Industrial Scrap

Customized collection and logistics for metal scrap, pallets, and high-volume recyclable byproducts.

How It Works

  1. 1. Schedule

    Tell us what you need recycled, and we build the right pickup or drop off plan.

  2. 2. Collect & Sort

    Materials are separated and prepared using proven systems to maximize recovery value.

  3. 3. Repurpose
